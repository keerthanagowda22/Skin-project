General skin health

Nutrition & Wellness for Healthy Skin

A dedicated Diet Plan Module recommends food habits and nutrition guidelines based on the identified skin condition, promoting holistic health management.

Balanced Nutrition

Choose the right vitamins, minerals, and food groups that support skin repair, reduce inflammation, and promote glowing skin.

Meditation & Yoga

Learn gentle yoga poses and relaxation techniques designed to support circulation, reduce stress, and complement your skin care routine.

Skin-Supporting Foods

  • Leafy greens, berries, and fatty fish for antioxidants and omega-3s.
  • Probiotic-rich yogurt and fermented foods to support gut-skin balance.
  • Hydrating fruits like cucumber, watermelon, and citrus.
  • Whole grains, nuts, and seeds for sustained energy and skin repair.

Focus on a colorful plate with lean protein and healthy fats to reduce skin inflammation and improve overall radiance.

Relaxation Practices

  • Deep breathing exercises to calm the nervous system.
  • Gentle yoga flows like Cat-Cow and Child's Pose for circulation.
  • Meditation sessions to lower stress hormones and improve sleep.
  • Mindful grounding routines to support emotional and skin health.

These practices help reduce stress-related skin flare-ups while supporting healthier habits from the inside out.

Daily Nutrition Habits

  • Drink plenty of water and herbal teas throughout the day.
  • Limit refined sugar, fried foods, and processed snacks.
  • Include fresh whole foods at every meal for sustained energy.
  • Track simple nutrition goals and stay consistent with meal timing.

Healthy routines make it easier to keep your skin glowing, especially when combined with rest and gentle movement.
